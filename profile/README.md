# Bandcamp Artist Support

Bandcamp is a music platform that connects independent artists with listeners through music releases, merchandise, and direct fan support. Artists can create a customizable storefront, present their discography, communicate with their audience, and manage sales from a central artist account.

## Overview

Bandcamp provides artists with a direct way to present music and merchandise to an audience. Artist pages bring releases, artwork, track previews, purchase options, and fan activity together in a single storefront.

The service supports different release formats and gives artists control over pricing and presentation. Fans can discover music, follow artists, purchase releases, and contribute to the continued work of musicians they appreciate.

## Essential Artist Tools

| Feature | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Artist Storefront | Present music, artwork, merchandise, and artist information on a customizable page. |
| Music Releases | Organize albums, EPs, and individual tracks for listeners. |
| Digital Music Sales | Offer music through configurable purchase options. |
| Physical Merchandise | Present eligible physical products, including records and apparel. |

| Embedded Players | Share music through customizable players on supported websites. |
| Artist Subscriptions | Offer recurring support with subscription features when available to the artist. |

## Music Sales and Artist Support

Bandcamp is designed around direct transactions between artists and their audience. Artists can set prices for eligible releases, choose supported purchase formats, and let listeners contribute above a stated price where that option is available.

Artist revenue depends on the type of sale, applicable platform fees, payment processing, and other relevant charges. Review the current terms before setting prices or planning revenue.

## Release Management Workflow

1. **Create an Artist Account**  
   Set up an artist profile and provide the information needed for the storefront.

2. **Customize the Artist Page**  
   Add artwork, a biography, links, and other relevant artist details.

3. **Prepare a Release**  
   Enter release information, upload supported audio files, and arrange the tracklist.

4. **Configure Sales Options**  
   Select available formats, pricing, and other purchase settings.

5. **Publish and Promote**  
   Make the release available and share it with followers and listeners.

6. **Review Audience Activity**  
   Use available sales information and fan engagement tools to assess how releases perform.

## Merchandise and Physical Releases

Artists can use Bandcamp to present eligible physical products alongside music. Depending on the product and account setup, these may include vinyl records, CDs, cassettes, apparel, and other merchandise.

Product availability, shipping arrangements, taxes, and order requirements depend on the seller's settings and destination.

## Fan Engagement

Bandcamp combines music discovery with tools that help artists maintain contact with listeners. Followers can receive release notifications, explore artist discographies, and interact with music through supported features.

Artist subscriptions can provide another way for eligible creators to receive recurring support. Available options depend on the artist's account and subscription configuration.

## Installation / Setup

Bandcamp is accessed through its official website rather than a conventional desktop installer. Use the GET button below to open the configured project destination.

To start using Bandcamp:

1. Open Bandcamp in a supported browser.
2. Create or sign in to an artist account.
3. Complete the artist profile and storefront details.
4. Add a music release or eligible merchandise.
5. Configure pricing and available purchase options.
6. Review the storefront and publish when ready.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Suggested Check |
| --- | --- |
| Artist page is incomplete | Review profile details, artwork, and storefront settings. |
| Release cannot be published | Check required fields and current upload specifications. |
| Payment setup is unavailable | Review account eligibility and supported payment configuration. |
| Fans cannot find a release | Confirm publication status, artist page details, and sharing links. |
| Merchandise settings are missing | Check product eligibility and the available seller tools. |
| Subscription setup is unavailable | Review the artist subscription requirements in the Help Center. |
